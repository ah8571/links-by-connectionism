AI Processing Disclosure

Last updated: September 5, 2026

Several FreeSurf tools use artificial-intelligence models to produce their output. This page explains the AI models we use, who runs them, what we send to them, and what happens to the results. It complements our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

1. Where AI models run

We choose the AI infrastructure that best fits each tool, and we may switch between providers over time. AI processing currently runs on one or more of the following:

  • Together AI — a hosted, serverless GPU inference provider (used for most of our models today).
  • OpenRouter — a hosted inference aggregator (used for some tools and fallbacks).
  • Self-hosted RunPod — our own rented GPU infrastructure (used for specific models we run ourselves).

We generally prefer open-source models (for example, open-weight text, vision, speech, and text-to-speech models). We use a closed-source model only when it is necessary to achieve an acceptable result for a particular feature.

2. What we send to an AI provider

Depending on the tool you are using, the inputs you provide are sent to the active provider's infrastructure solely to generate the result you requested:

ToolInputs sent to the AI provider
Natural ReaderText you ask to be read aloud
TranscriberAudio you ask to be transcribed
Calorie TrackerFood photos and/or food descriptions
English TutorYour spoken or typed replies, and context used to generate a correction

Your inputs are shared only with the provider(s) running the model for that request. They are used strictly to produce the output; we do not use them to sell to or profile you, and we instruct providers not to use submitted content to train their models.

3. What happens to the results

The result returned by the AI provider is delivered back to you in the app. Depending on how you are using the tool, results may be:

  • Stored locally on your device (for example, generated audio or saved recordings), and
  • Associated with your account and stored in our database if you are signed in, so your history and content can sync across devices.

Where a result is associated with a user, it is tied to your account and subject to the same access, retention, and deletion rights described in our Privacy Policy.

4. Model training

Content you submit is never used to train or fine-tune our models, and we do not permit the providers we use to train on it for their own benefit.

5. Data minimization

We send only the information required for the specific request, we do not combine your AI inputs with unrelated personal data, and we rely on providers that delete or do not retain submitted content beyond what is required to fulfill the request. If you have questions, contact us at support@freesurf.tools.
